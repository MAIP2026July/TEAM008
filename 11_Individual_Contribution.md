## 3. Collective Team Contribution

DealFit emerged from a collaborative idea-selection process rather than from a single pre-decided concept. Team members discussed multiple project directions, compared how well the alternatives would work as a team-based AI product-management project, and progressively narrowed the discussion toward the creator-economy problem.

One alternative concept already available within the team was also discussed, but the team decided not to proceed with it because it was better suited to an individual project than to the intended collaborative assignment. The team ultimately converged on DealFit and jointly established the foundation for:

- the DealFit product title;
- the creator sponsorship-screening problem;
- the independent-creator target direction;
- the decision-support rather than full-automation philosophy;
- the approved topic submission;
- the high-level product scope used throughout the project.

As the project progressed, team members continued to support planning, review, coordination and discussion, while the detailed PM artifact integration, prototype/testing cycle and repository consolidation became more concentrated.
